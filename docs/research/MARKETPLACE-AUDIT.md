# Detailed model marketplace audit

Checked 5 November 2026. Prices are the displayed USD listing prices, not a quote
or an acquisition. No model was purchased. A marketplace listing is evidence
that a seller offers an asset, not independent proof of survey accuracy or ownership.

## Singapore circuit

[Alpha92's Marina Bay Street Circuit](https://www.cgtrader.com/3d-models/exterior/stadium/marina-bay-street-circuit-f1-singapore-grand-prix)
is offered at **$299.99** under a Royalty Free licence. The listing supplies OBJ
(90.8 MB), FBX (32.8 MB), MAX and PNG textures (45.4 MB). Its preview shows
trackside fencing, grandstands, lighting and distinctive buildings. The FBX has
CGTrader technical checks, but these do not establish geographic fidelity or
the provenance of the model and textures.

Crucially, the description excludes backgrounds and scenes used in previews.
There is no verified capture date, geographic reference, city coverage polygon,
or independent provenance. It is a **candidate for detailed circuit structures**,
not a verified photogrammetric city acquisition. Check the actual deliverable,
layout year, underlying rights and browser-delivery terms before purchase. Do
not distribute the source model in this public Git repository.

## Singapore photographic city candidate

[TurboCG's Singapore 3D Scan City](https://www.artstation.com/marketplace/p/M3gmo/singapore-3d-scan-city-3d-model)
lists FBX (3.2 GB compressed), Cinema 4D and textures (4.7 GB compressed), with
up to 16K texture dimensions. Displayed prices are **$183.27 Standard** and
**$244.77 Extended Commercial**. The Standard listing limits the commercial
project to 2,000 sales or 20,000 views; the extended listing removes those
project/view limits. Neither description alone establishes public raw-asset
redistribution rights.

The public description does not identify the original capture provider, date,
survey accuracy or georeferenced bounds. The word “scan” and large texture
dimensions cannot establish those facts. Keep this as an **unverified candidate**,
not a substitute for an independently licensed survey. Regional research has
additional checks on the visible coverage and alternative sources.

## Selection rule

Prefer a named survey producer and an exact circuit coverage check. Inspect
public previews as previews only. Do not extract mesh/texture payloads from
proprietary viewers, treat game-ripped uploads as licensed because an uploader
selected CC BY, or present a paid model as already integrated.
